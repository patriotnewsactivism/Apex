## What this session did not attempt, and why

- **No live Cloud Run, Supabase, OpenRouter-account, or AWS access existed in this sandboxed session.** Every finding above that says "requires live verification" is exactly that — not a gap in effort, a gap in credentials this session was never given and should not have been given for a sandboxed audit pass.
- **No merge of Apex-Stream's pending PRs, and no production deploy of any kind.** Both would be visible, shared-state-changing actions on infrastructure this audit could not verify the effect of.
- **No forced unification of the two systems' security/tool-permission models.** They protect genuinely different things (a single-operator business workforce vs. a compliance-grade evidence chain); a shared vocabulary should follow a real integration need, not precede one.
- **No rewrite of Apex-Stream's AWS-era documentation content.** Marking it historical was safe and honest; inventing the "correct" GCP-native replacement architecture to fill the gap would not have been — that decision belongs to whoever owns the compliance requirements behind the evidence chain.
